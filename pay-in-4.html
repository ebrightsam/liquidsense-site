Pay in 4: when does each payment hit?

Buy now, pay later splits a purchase into four payments, usually two weeks apart. See each one, its date, and the paycheck that has to cover it.

A missed payment can mean a late fee and, with some lenders, a hit to your credit. Plan for all four before you buy.
