Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. LiquidSense is made in Flagstaff, Arizona (“we”).

The short version

The bills, cards, loans, income and balances you enter stay on your device. We don’t have accounts, we don’t ask for bank logins, and we don’t run analytics or advertising tools in the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you type into LiquidSense is stored only in the app’s storage on your phone or tablet. We can’t see it. Deleting the app, or using More › Erase all data, removes it.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device. No reminder content is sent to us or to a notification server.

Purchases

If you buy LiquidSense Pro, payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your card details. To check whether you’ve bought Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts from the store. RevenueCat’s handling of that data is described in its privacy policy.

The website and calculator

The free calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us. This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

Children

LiquidSense isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and post the new version here.

Contact

Questions or requests: info@liquidsensestudio.com.
