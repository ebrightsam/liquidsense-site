Short description

LiquidSense shows where your money flows and the day it runs short, before it does. No bank login, no subscription: $9 once, in English and Spanish.

Long description

Most budgeting apps assume a steady paycheck and want your bank password. LiquidSense is built for everyone else: people paid every two weeks while rent is monthly, gig and freelance workers, and anyone juggling credit cards and Pay in 4.

Add your balance, paychecks, bills and card payments once. LiquidSense lays them on one 45-day timeline, finds the first day you’d go below zero, and shows exactly what to skip or move to cover it, starting with wants. Liquid, the home screen, draws every dollar flowing from paychecks into needs, debt, wants and savings.

Everything stays on the phone: no bank connection, no account, no servers, no ads. It works fully in English or Spanish, and Pro costs $9 once instead of a monthly subscription.
